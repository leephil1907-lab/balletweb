How it works

Understand the security model before you use it.

Ballet's REAL Series is a physical bearer-style cold-storage product. The card itself is non-electronic; the security model depends on the concealed access components and on the owner protecting the physical wallet and credentials.

FAQ

Questions worth answering first.

What is the difference between the standard and Gold REAL Series?

They use the same underlying design. The standard version is 304 stainless steel; the Gold Edition uses 304 stainless steel with a 24K gold-plated finish.

Does the physical card contain electronics?

The REAL Series is a non-electronic cold-storage product. See the technical specifications for the exact construction and security components.

Can Ballet recover my crypto if I lose the card?

No. Ballet describes the REAL Series as non-custodial. Protect the card and the credentials required to access and transfer assets.

How do I contact support?

Use the gold chat button at the bottom-right. It opens the real Smartsupp support session, where your message is delivered to the support team and their reply is returned to you in the same conversation.
