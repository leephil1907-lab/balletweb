FAQ

Questions people ask before storing crypto.

Does Ballet store my private key?

The Ballet security model is designed around self-custody. Review the dedicated private-key and 2FKG material for the exact generation and transfer model.

Is cold storage suitable for beginners?

The experience is designed to reduce setup complexity, but users still need to understand that a physical bearer asset must be protected like cash or other valuable property.

How do I verify a product?

Use the verification route before loading the wallet and only purchase through authorized channels.

Where can I get help?

Use the support chat button in the lower-right corner. The site now keeps a local support assistant visible whenever the hosted live-chat provider is unavailable.
