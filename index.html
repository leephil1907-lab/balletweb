How it works

Simple on the outside.
Serious underneath.

The physical card presents a deposit address. The protected components needed to spend are kept on the card and only assembled into a full private key on the user's device when required.

01 · THE CARD

Scan & receive

Use the visible deposit QR code to send assets to the cold storage without connecting the card to a network.

02 · KEY ASSEMBLY

Two parts become one

The protected private-key components are combined through the BIP38 process when you need to move assets out.

03 · PRODUCTION

Two-factor generation

Ballet's published security model separates key components during manufacturing so the complete private key is not created at the factory.

FAQ

Questions before you store.

A few of the questions people ask before using physical cold storage.

Does Ballet store my private key?

The published Ballet security model is designed so the company does not have the complete private key. Review the private-key security material for the full 2FKG explanation.

Is cold storage suitable for beginners?

The product is designed to reduce setup complexity, but the physical card should be protected like cash or another valuable bearer asset.

What assets can the REAL Series support?

Ballet states that the REAL Series supports more than 1,000 cryptocurrencies and NFTs through the Ballet Crypto ecosystem.

What happens if I lose the card?

Physical possession is critical. If the card and the credentials needed to authorize transactions are lost or destroyed, access to assets can be permanently lost.

Where can I get help?

Use the live support launcher on this site or visit Ballet Support for documentation and customer assistance.
