FAQ

Find answers to some of the most common questions.

Does Ballet store my private key?
No, Ballet never stores your private key. You are the only one who can ever generate the actual full private key, and only when you transfer out funds for the first time. Learn more about Ballet's Private Key Security.
Is Ballet Cold Storage safe for beginners?
Yes! Ballet Cold Storage is designed with beginners in mind. Because it stays completely offline, your crypto isn't exposed to online hacking risks. There's no need to set up an account, manage seed phrases, or have technical knowledge—making it easy to use right from the start. Just treat your Ballet card like cash or gold: whoever holds it controls the funds, so keep it safe and private.
What currencies does Ballet Cold Storage support?
The REAL Series Cold Storage wallet supports over 1,000 cryptocurrencies and NFTs, while Ballet Cold Storage Coins are designed to support one currency (Bitcoin or XRP). Explore our options and choose the solution that best fits your needs.
Why should I use cold storage for my crypto instead of an exchange?
Exchanges are centralized platforms susceptible to hacking, financial collapse, government seizures, and more—any of which can put your funds at serious risk. Keeping crypto on an exchange means trusting them to keep your assets safe and to let you access them when you want to. Cold storage, on the other hand, is decentralized and offline, making it much less vulnerable to security breaches and giving you more control over your crypto assets.
Why should I choose Ballet Cold Storage over other hardware wallets?
Ballet Cold Storage wallets have several advantages over other crypto wallets: • Fully offline — safe from hackers and malware. • 100% self-custodial — no one else has access to your private keys. • Effortless — just scan, verify, and go. • Gift-ready — no account, password, or seed phrase set up. • Seamlessly convenient — buy crypto, sell, swap, and more with the Ballet Crypto App.
